🚀 Launching: AWS Security Weekly Radar

Every Monday, one short read with what changed in AWS security last week. The week of September 30 had 51 security items, including 7 new security bulletins and 19 updates to older ones. Nobody has time to read all of that, so I built a Claude Code skill that reads it for you.

🌎 Your language:
English, Português or Español. You choose at setup, and the whole report arrives in that language.

🧭 What lands in your inbox and Slack:
• The headline of the week
• 3 to 7 highlights, each with priority, why it matters and one suggested action
• Also released: the rest of the relevant news, split into News and Blog posts
• Good to know: every new CVE and security bulletin in one compact table

🛡️ Trust by design:
The agent chooses and explains. Scripts guarantee the facts. Titles, dates and links come only from official AWS sources, and a validator blocks the send if anything does not match.

🗺️ What comes next:
Monthly, quarterly and YTD views in January. Kiro support in February. Personas and service filters in March. LGPD, BACEN and PCI DSS mapping in May, after legal review.
